# Design & Branding

## Philosophy

**Keep it simple. Keep it real.**

We're not a corporate brand. Our design should reflect what we are: authentic, human, unpretentious. Think clean, minimal, genuine.

## Visual Identity

### Logo

**Concept**: Something simple that represents Budapest + running + community

Ideas to explore:
- Minimal line drawing of Budapest landmarks (Parliament, bridges) with a running figure
- Simple runner silhouette with city outline
- Just text-based ("FunRuns Budapest" in clean font) — sometimes simple is best

**The key**: It should feel like it was made by friends, not a design agency.

### Colors

We're going minimal. These don't need to be perfect—they're just guides.

- **Primary**: Dark gray/charcoal (#2C2C2C) — grounded, real
- **Accent**: Warm orange (#E07856) — friendly, approachable, energetic
- **Secondary**: Light cream/off-white (#F5F1ED) — easy on the eyes

**Use sparingly.** Our strength is simplicity, not color explosion.

### Typography

- **Headings**: Clean, modern sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Helvetica, system fonts)
- **Body**: Same family, easy to read

**Rule**: If it looks fancy or designed, we probably overdid it.

## How to Use This

When creating social posts, flyers, or materials:
- Use 1-2 colors max
- White space is your friend
- Photos should be real (not stock images)
- Keep text honest and direct

## Examples

**Good**: A simple black text flyer with event details and an orange accent line

**Bad**: Gradient backgrounds, multiple fonts, motivational quotes everywhere

---

**This is a living guide.** As we create things together, we'll refine this. The goal is always: authentic, not polished.
