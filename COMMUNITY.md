# How We Do Things Here

## Our Values

**Authenticity** - Be yourself. Bring your real self to our runs.

**Kindness** - We're all here to connect. That means meeting people where they are.

**Inclusivity** - Every pace, every fitness level, every background is welcome.

**Honesty** - If something isn't working, let's talk about it openly.

## Guidelines

### On Runs
- Show up on time (or let us know if you can't)
- Run your own pace—faster people, slower people, walkers—all good
- Chat when you want, run quietly when you want
- Look out for each other
- Have fun

### In the Community
- Respect people's boundaries and preferences
- No judgment about fitness, gear, background, or where you're at in life
- Disagreements happen—handle them with respect
- If someone feels excluded or uncomfortable, speak up

### Online
- Keep discussions real and constructive
- Share ideas openly, listen to feedback
- No spam, self-promotion, or gatekeeping

## If Something Doesn't Feel Right

Reach out directly or open an issue. We want to know what's not working so we can fix it together.

---

**This is a living document.** As the community grows, we'll update this based on what we learn together.
